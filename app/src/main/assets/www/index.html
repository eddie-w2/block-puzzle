How to play
01Drag pieces from the tray onto the grid. Every square has its own color. Full rows don't clear in this mode. 02To clear squares, drag across touching squares of the same color (up, down or sideways) and let go. Two or more make a link. Longer links score much more. 03Bring a link back to a square it already passed through to make a loop. A loop clears every square of that color on the board. 04Bonuses: a link of 6+ squares +100, 8+ +250, 10+ +500. Clearing the last square of a color +250 (loops add +25 for each extra square). A loop that sweeps 12+ squares +300. Emptying the board +1,000. 05You earn 1 credit for every 100 points. Spend credits in the Shop during a game on items that free up space. 06With Shake to drop on in Settings, shake your phone or swipe down fast on the board to make every square fall to the bottom of its column. 07The game ends when no piece fits, no two matching squares touch, and you have no items or drops left to try.
01Plays like Link: match colors to clear squares, with the same bonuses, credits and shop. 02Gravity is always on. As soon as you place a piece, its squares fall to the bottom of their columns. 03When a link clears squares, everything above them falls to fill the gap. Plan for squares landing next to new neighbors. 04The game ends when no piece fits, no two matching squares touch, and you have no items left to try.
01The board starts full of dots. There are no pieces to place. 02Drag across touching dots of one color (up, down or sideways) and let go to clear them. Each dot is worth 10 points. 03Close a loop to clear every dot of that color. The new dots that drop in won't be that color. 04Cleared dots leave gaps, the dots above fall, and new dots drop in from the top. 05You have 30 moves, and each link uses one. If nothing can be linked, the board reshuffles.
01Drag pieces from the tray onto the grid. 02Fill a whole row or column to clear it. Clearing several at once multiplies the points. 03Clear again within 3 moves to build a chain. Each link in the chain adds ×0.5 to your points. 04The game ends when none of your three pieces fits.

Turn on Undo in Settings to take back moves. Each undo brings new random pieces or dots.

Settings

Shake to drop works in Link mode (Gravity mode always drops): shake your phone, or swipe down fast on the board, and every square falls to the bottom of its column.
