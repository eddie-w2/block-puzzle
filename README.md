# Sector 8

A moody, rain-soaked cyberpunk block puzzle for Android. Drag pieces onto an 8×8 grid and clear them. Four main modes (Link, Gravity, Classic Dots, Classic Blocks) plus Rush and Daily Sector.

## Modes

**Link (main mode):** every square of a piece has its own color. Drag across touching squares of one color to clear them, or close a loop to clear that color from the whole board. Points earn credits (1 per 100 points) to spend in the in-game shop.

| Item | Price | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Block destroyer | 5 for 50 credits | Removes one square |
| Color wipe | 1 for 50 credits | Clears every square of one color |
| Shuffle | 1 for 25 credits | Moves every square to a random spot |

Bonuses reward long links, clearing the last square of a color, big loops and emptying the board. Turn on **Shake to drop** in Settings, then shake your phone or swipe down fast to make every square fall to the bottom.

**Gravity:** Link with gravity always on. Pieces fall to the bottom as soon as you place them, and squares drop into gaps left by links.

**Classic Dots:** the original dots game, endless. A full board, and new squares drop in as you clear. Uses the same credits, shop and items. End the game from the pause menu to save your score.

**Classic Blocks:** fill rows or columns to clear them, and chain clears for bonus points.

**Rush:** Classic Dots on a 60-second clock. Every loop adds 3 seconds.

**Daily Sector:** one board a day, the same for everyone, on Link's rules with no items or undo. One try per day.

An optional Undo button can be turned on in Settings.

## Install

Download the APK from [**Latest build**](../../releases/tag/latest) on your Android phone and open it. After that, the app updates itself.
